## Cross-Cutting Feature Themes

### Table-Stakes Features

- **Screen and/or webcam recording** — Capture video from screen, camera, or both simultaneously
- **Link-based sharing** — Generate shareable links; no login required for viewers
- **Transcription** — Auto-generate transcripts from audio
- **Cloud storage** — Persistent video library in cloud
- **Video player** — Customizable playback with controls
- **Mobile recording** — Mobile app for on-the-go capture
- **Search and organization** — Find videos by name, tag, or content
- **Basic analytics** — View count, watch time, engagement metrics
- **Export/download** — Save videos locally in common formats

### Differentiating Features

- **AI-powered editing** — Auto-cuts, silence removal, filler-word cleanup
- **Personalization engine** — Insert recipient details without re-recording
- **CRM integration** — Deep connections with Salesforce, HubSpot
- **Transcript-based editing** — Edit video by modifying text transcript
- **AI voice (Overdub)** — Generate missing sections via AI voice
- **Multi-track recording** — Separate audio tracks for professionals
- **Call-to-action overlays** — Interactive buttons in video
- **Show-note automation** — Auto-generate descriptions and chapters
- **Atlassian ecosystem** — Native Jira and Confluence integration
- **Sales pipeline attribution** — Track video impact on deals

### Underserved Areas / Opportunities

- **Open-source async video** — No strong open-source alternative
- **Privacy-first, self-hosted** — For organizations avoiding SaaS
- **Lightweight, minimal tool** — For teams rejecting feature bloat
- **Internal communication focus** — vs. sales/marketing bias
- **AI coaching during recording** — Real-time guidance on delivery
- **Semantic video search** — Find moments within videos (not just files)
- **Cross-platform clip generation** — Auto-create vertical/square/landscape for social
- **Accessibility-first design** — Captions, transcripts, audio descriptions native
- **Collaborative editing** — Multiple users edit same video simultaneously
- **Video-to-blog conversion** — Auto-generate blog posts from videos

### AI-Augmentation Candidates

- **Filler-word and silence removal** — Current: manual editing. Better: ML auto-remove in real-time
- **AI coaching overlay** — Current: none. Better: real-time suggestions on pacing, tone, clarity
- **Semantic video search** — Current: keyword search. Better: ML find moments within videos by meaning
- **Personalized variants** — Current: manual re-recording. Better: ML clone voice/face; insert context
- **Intelligent follow-up routing** — Current: manual. Better: ML detect viewer drop-off; auto-followup
- **Auto-chapter generation** — Current: manual. Better: ML identify segment breaks; auto-chapter
- **Engagement analysis** — Current: basic heatmaps. Better: ML analyze tone, participation, dominance
- **Auto-transcription improvement** — Current: basic accuracy. Better: ML learn speaker vocab; improve over time
- **Recommended clips** — Current: manual selection. Better: ML identify most shareable moments

## Legal & IP Summary

**WebRTC and media capture:** WebRTC is an open standard (no patent concerns). Individual implementations may have patent implications; review before building competing capture technology.

**AI voice and face cloning:** Sendspark's personalization and Descript's Overdub use AI voice synthesis. These techniques may be patent-encumbered; independent legal review recommended before implementing similar capabilities.

**Transcript-based editing:** Descript's transcript-first approach may have patent protection. Organizations implementing similar workflows should conduct patent review.

**No material was omitted due to copyright uncertainty.** All sources were publicly available product documentation.
